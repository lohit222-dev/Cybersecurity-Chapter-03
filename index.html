🇮🇳 3.2 Why Does India Need Strong Cyber Laws?

Indian Context

With millions of daily UPI, online banking, and e-commerce transactions, India faces unique cybersecurity challenges:

1
Rising Cybercrimes: Online financial fraud, identity theft, and phishing are growing rapidly every year.
2
National Security: Cyber terrorism and hacking attempts on defense, power grids, and government websites threaten sovereignty.
3
E-Commerce Growth: Ensures public trust in online shopping, digital payments, and legally binding e-contracts.
4
Digital India Initiative: Paperless e-governance (Aadhaar, DigiLocker, e-Filing) relies on a strong legal framework.
5
User Protection: Safeguards citizens from cyberstalking, harassment, deepfakes, and financial fraud.
